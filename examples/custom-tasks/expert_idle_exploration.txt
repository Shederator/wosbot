Title: Empty the Exploration bag using idle mode
Status: Editable Task Builder example.
When: Run at startup and then twice a day after validation on your account.

How to test:
1. Stop the normal task queue after initialization.
2. Open Task Builder and import expert_idle_exploration.json.
3. Inspect or execute each node, then save and re-import the JSON.

Flow:
- Expand the dashboard.
- Swipe up twice.
- Go to Trek Supplies.
- Open and close the bag preview.
- Open Tundra Trek and select Idle.
- Confirm the quantity and return to the world screen.
