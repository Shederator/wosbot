# Live Fishing Tournament Species Sighting Log

Source: `C:\Bearguard\human_play_frames\` — 350 real gameplay PNG frames (~350ms apart) from one
continuous dive. Reviewed via 18 contact-sheet grids (all 350 frames, 20/sheet) for an exhaustive pass,
then individual frames pulled at full resolution for identification and cropping.

**Scope correction:** only frames ~78–211 are actual underwater dive footage. Frames 1–77 are the
Whiteout Survival base/world map (pre-dive) and the "3…2…1…GO" countdown; frames 212–350 are the
post-dive Haul/rewards screen and the static Fishing Tournament menu (rod re-cast over and over,
no new sightings). So the exhaustive pass covers all ~134 frames that actually contain fish, which is
all of the source data that exists for this task — nothing was skipped.

**Ground truth bonus:** this dive's Haul screen (frames 221–226) and four "Congratulations on earning a
new Guide" unlock cards (frames 213–220) gave direct, non-inferred identifications for several species:
**Bonefish**, **Nutcracker**, **Horn**, **Tin Soldier** (the last three are Trident trinkets, not fish —
excluded from this log). The Haul also confirmed 9 exact item icons caught this run, several of which
matched fish seen swimming throughout the dive and let me cross-check color/shape against catalog.md
with much higher confidence than eyeballing alone.

Total distinct species/creatures cataloged: **23** (22 fish/invertebrates + the sea turtle), plus one
confirmed hazard (the pike) and one flagged "worth checking" oddity (a remora-like sucker-headed fish).

## 01 — Bonefish
- Examples: `species_01_bonefish/example_right_facing.png`, `example_left_facing.png`
- Description: Elongated trout/salmon-shaped fish, olive-tan body with reddish vertical bars along the
  flank, forked tail, orange-tipped fins, prominent eye. **The single most common fish in the dive** —
  present in nearly every frame from ~85m to ~250m depth.
- Speed: Medium — cruises steadily across screen, covers roughly a third of screen height every few
  frames; not a darter, not sluggish.
- Almanac match: **Confirmed** — directly named via the in-game "Congratulations on earning a new Guide"
  unlock card (frame 214, shows "Bonefish", 3-4★) and matches the Haul screen's #1 item exactly. Closest
  catalog.md text description is row 3 slot 2, "orange salmon-shape — CAUGHT" (Winter, 1★) — the star
  tier printed in catalog.md doesn't line up with what the Guide card/Haul icon show (3★ vs 4★ vs 1★
  respectively), so I can't be certain this is the *same* almanac slot vs. a separate similar-looking
  entry, but the species identity itself (Bonefish) is confirmed by the game, not a guess.
- Frame appearances: ~70 of ~134 dive frames (rough count).

## 19 — PIKE — CONFIRMED HAZARD
- Examples: `species_19_pike_HAZARD/example_1.png` (the exact frame-107 hazard sighting),
  `example_2.png` (a second sighting later in the dive, frame 110)

  more predatory-looking than every other fish in the dive (long jaw line, sharper head profile).

  multiple depths, not a one-off.
- Speed: Medium-fast, and notably angles toward the player/hook rather than just drifting past — the
  behavior that likely causes the shield hits.
- Almanac match: **Confirmed** — row 6 slot 4, "pike-shape — CAUGHT". Matches matt's description exactly

- Frame appearances: ~8 frames (at least two separate encounters: ~106-109 and ~110).
