# Land of Heroes (Labyrinth) — Combat Mechanics Research Brief

Compiled 2026-08-10. Sources: whiteoutsurvival.wiki, heaven-guardian.com, theriagames.com,
wos.h5joy-games.com, mone.gg, outof.games, buffbuff.com, ldshop.gg, whiteoutsurvivalhandbook.com,
and general web search aggregation. **No live Reddit thread or YouTube transcript was directly
retrievable** — Reddit search returned no indexed r/whiteoutsurvival threads on this exact topic,
and the one YouTube video fetched (`Labyrinth Guide [gameplay, rewards, tips and tricks]`,
youtube.com/watch?v=LzFjiwl-aUc) only returned page chrome, not a transcript, so it could not be
used as a source here. Everything below is fan-wiki / guide-site sourced.

**Important caveat on independence:** several of these guide sites return *identical* numeric troop
ratios (e.g. "60/40/0", "54/3/43", "50/20/30") and identical hero call-outs (Greg, Mia). This
strongly suggests most of them are copying/aggregating from one original source rather than being
independently verified — treat the exact ratio numbers as "one primary source, widely mirrored,"
not "5 independent confirmations." The *structural* claims (normalization, which stat pools apply,
squad-concentration strategy) are corroborated across genuinely distinct-looking sites, so those are
higher confidence.

## 1. Does troop tier/quality matter, or are troops normalized?

**Confirmed, cross-checked across ~6 independent-looking sources:** In Land of Heroes (and every
Labyrinth zone except Sunday's Gaia Heart), the game issues **standardized Lv.10 troops via the
"Labyrinth Explorers"** — your own troop tier/research level is irrelevant. This matches your
in-battle observation of both sides showing "Lv 10.0."

- "You will use Lv.10 Troops provided by the Labyrinth Explorers for challenges in Land of the
  Brave, Cave of Monsters, Glowstone Mine, Earthlab, and Dark Forge." — [Whiteout Survival Wiki](https://www.whiteoutsurvival.wiki/events/the-labyrinth/), [theriagames.com](https://theriagames.com/guide/whiteout-survival-the-labyrinth/)
- "In every zone except Gaia Heart, everyone will be on equal footing in terms of raw troop
  strength... this clever design ensures a level playing field." — [outof.games](https://outof.games/realms/whiteoutsurvival/guides/468-the-labyrinth-in-whiteout-survival/), [ldshop.gg](https://www.ldshop.gg/blog/guide/whiteout-survival-labyrinth-guide.html)
- Gaia Heart (Sunday zone) is the explicit, single exception: player's own troops are used there,
  and higher-tier (Fire Crystal/Helios) troops matter. — [outof.games](https://outof.games/realms/whiteoutsurvival/guides/468-the-labyrinth-in-whiteout-survival/)

**One nuance that is NOT normalized:** troop **type ratio** (Infantry/Lancer/Marksman split) stays
fully player-controlled even though troop *level* is fixed at 10 — this is confirmed separately
(see Q2/Q3 below), not a contradiction of normalization.

Confidence: **high** — this is the single most consistently repeated claim across every source
fetched, including the game's own in-app text you already have.

## 2. What actually determines combat power, if not troop tier?

Confirmed stat pool for Land of Heroes, stated near-verbatim across multiple sites and matching the
in-game text you quoted:

- **Heroes** (level/rank)
- **Hero Gear**
- **Hero Exclusive Gear** (the hero's unique weapon)

— [heaven-guardian.com](https://heaven-guardian.com/whiteout-survival-labyrinth-guide/), [outof.games](https://outof.games/realms/whiteoutsurvival/guides/468-the-labyrinth-in-whiteout-survival/), [theriagames.com](https://theriagames.com/guide/whiteout-survival-the-labyrinth/)

**(d) Troop composition ratio (Infantry/Lancer/Marksman):** Confirmed as still meaningful and
player-adjustable, functioning as the classic rock-paper-scissors counter system even though troop
*level* is fixed. Sources give a default/baseline of roughly 33/33/33 or 50/20/30, with
zone/stage-specific tuning recommended (e.g., stage 10 skews toward ~53/27/20 Infantry-heavy).
— [wos.h5joy-games.com](https://wos.h5joy-games.com/guides/the-labyrinth-guide/), [general aggregation](https://www.topuplive.com/news/whiteout-survival-troops-guide.html)

**(e) Troop count as an HP pool:** Not explicitly confirmed or denied by name in any source fetched
— no site directly addressed whether deployed troop *count* (vs. just ratio) scales an HP pool in
this zone. This is a plausible inference from general WoS combat mechanics (troop count = force
size) but **I could not confirm this specifically for Land of Heroes** — flag as unconfirmed.

**Chief gear, pet buffs, city/troop research:** Explicitly stated to **NOT** apply in this zone —
confirmed by two independently-phrased sources:
- "Chief Gear, Pets, and Research do not directly contribute here. Hero development is the
  exclusive focus." — [heaven-guardian.com](https://heaven-guardian.com/whiteout-survival-labyrinth-guide/)
- "Chief Gear, Pet stats, and Research Center tech do not affect Land of the Brave combat. The zone
  specifically uses hero-related expedition stats only." — [outof.games](https://outof.games/realms/whiteoutsurvival/guides/468-the-labyrinth-in-whiteout-survival/)

This lines up with the general Labyrinth design pattern other sources describe, where **each zone
draws from a different stat pool** (Land of Heroes = heroes; Cave of Monsters = pets; Glowstone
Mine = charms/gems; Earthlab = research/tech; Dark Forge = chief gear) — so pets/tech/chief-gear
matter *elsewhere in the Labyrinth*, just not in Land of Heroes specifically.
— [general aggregation, topuplive.com](https://www.topuplive.com/news/whiteout-survival-troops-guide.html)

**Hero skills / star-ascension level / pets attached to heroes:** No source explicitly broke out
hero *skill level* or *star/ascension rank* as a separate confirmed lever distinct from "hero
level" — they're likely folded into "hero strength" generically. **Not separately confirmed**,
treat as a reasonable inference (skills and ascension are core hero power stats generally) rather
than a sourced claim.

Confidence: **high** on the three named stat pools and the chief-gear/pet/research exclusion
(2 independently worded sources agree); **medium** on troop ratio mattering (specific, consistent,
but same-numbers-everywhere pattern noted above); **unconfirmed** on troop count as HP pool and on
hero skill/star level as a distinct lever.

## 3. Highest-leverage levers to clear harder Land of Heroes stages, ranked

No source gave an explicit ranked list, but synthesizing the repeated advice pattern across
sites (guides converge on the same order of operations even if not stated as a numbered list):

1. **Hero Exclusive Gear (weapon) upgrades** — repeatedly called out as the standout priority:
   "Upgrading their exclusive gear is paramount, significantly boosting their abilities and
   providing crucial advantages." — [whiteoutdata-adjacent aggregation](https://www.gamsgo.com/blog/whiteout-survival-hero-tier-list) / [outof.games](https://outof.games/realms/whiteoutsurvival/guides/468-the-labyrinth-in-whiteout-survival/)
2. **Hero Gear** — the second explicit named stat pool; multiple guides specifically recommend
   *reallocating* gear away from support heroes (Greg, Mia — see Q4) onto your primary
   damage/tank heroes rather than spreading it evenly.
3. **Hero level/strength itself** (generic leveling) — named as part of the stat pool but treated
   as more of a baseline than the differentiator; gear is what guides emphasize as the marginal win.
4. **Troop composition ratio tuned to counter the enemy comp** — treated as real but secondary
   optimization, adjusted per-stage/per-zone rather than a one-time setup.
5. **Concentrating your best heroes into fewer, stronger squads rather than spreading 9 heroes
   evenly** — this came up independently in two guide breakdowns (mone.gg and the aggregated
   theriagames-derived guidance): "concentrated approach proves more effective than creating three
   average teams, as most players lack nine fully upgraded heroes with premium gear."
   — [mone.gg](https://www.mone.gg/blog/whiteout-survival/labyrinth-guide.html)

Sources broadly agree the underlying answer to your framing is **yes** — it is essentially "level
and gear your best heroes, pick a troop ratio that counters what you're fighting" — with hero
exclusive gear singled out as the single most emphasized lever.

Confidence: **medium** — the priority order is a synthesis across sources, not a single source's
explicit numbered ranking, but the *component* claims (exclusive gear = top priority; concentrate
gear on fewer heroes) each show up more than once.

## 4. Best heroes and squad-splitting strategy (up to 3 squads)

**Consensus recommendation on splitting (not spreading evenly):** Concentrate your strongest
heroes/gear into Squads 1 and 2; treat Squad 3 as the leftover/lower-priority squad. This was
stated multiple ways across sources:

- "Give your strongest heroes and equipment to Squads 1 and 2." — [aggregated guide search result citing theriagames/heaven-guardian family]
- "Concentrated approach proves more effective than creating three average teams... rather than
  distributing resources evenly across nine heroes." — [mone.gg](https://www.mone.gg/blog/whiteout-survival/labyrinth-guide.html)
- One guide even frames Squad 3 explicitly as a "non-core squad for free-to-play players" with
  limited battlefield impact. — [wos.h5joy-games.com](https://wos.h5joy-games.com/guides/the-labyrinth-guide/)

**Specific named hero recommendations (repeated near-identically across several sites, so likely
one primary source widely mirrored — treat numbers as indicative, not gospel):**

- **Squad 1:** Best Infantry hero + Best Lancer hero + **Greg** (support), ratio ~60% Infantry /
  40% Lancer / 0% Marksman. Notably, guides recommend **stripping Greg's own Hero Gear off him**
  so it can go on your primary Infantry/Lancer hero instead.
- **Squad 2:** Second-best Infantry hero + Best Marksman hero + **Mia** (support), ratio ~54%
  Infantry / 3% Lancer / 43% Marksman (Mia's cross-troop bonus lets this squad run light on
  Lancers). Same gear-stripping logic applies to Mia.
- **Squad 3:** Remaining/leftover heroes, ratio ~50% Infantry / 20% Lancer / 30% Marksman — treated
  as the lower-priority squad, especially for F2P accounts without 9 fully-geared heroes.

— consistently repeated across [theriagames.com](https://theriagames.com/guide/whiteout-survival-the-labyrinth/), [heaven-guardian.com](https://heaven-guardian.com/whiteout-survival-labyrinth-guide/), [wos.h5joy-games.com](https://wos.h5joy-games.com/guides/the-labyrinth-guide/), and general aggregated search results.

**No source** gave a "tank squad vs. damage squad" framing explicitly — the actual pattern found is
closer to "strongest squad first, second squad built around a specific support hero (Mia), third
squad is overflow," which partially answers your question but isn't the tank/DPS split you asked
about as a named strategy.

Confidence: **medium-low** on the specific hero names and exact percentages (numbers repeat
suspiciously identically across sites, suggesting one un-attributed original source rather than
independent testing by multiple guide authors) — **medium** on the higher-level structural claim
("concentrate best heroes in squads 1-2, squad 3 is overflow") since that shows up in differently-
worded form across at least 2 more clearly distinct sources (mone.gg and wos.h5joy-games.com).
